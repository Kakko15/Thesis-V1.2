Objective 2 · Comparative Performance Analysis

Does retrieval beat parametric memory?

Forty faculty-validated queries, each answered twice — once by unaugmented Gemini relying on parametric memory, once by the RAG pipeline constrained to the CCSICT vector archive — and scored against the panel's reference answers with Ragas Answer Correctness. The pooled answer is yes. The stratum the paper actually quotes is less certain, and this page shows why.

The effect, split by what the corpus could support

Every query carries a corpus_coverage stratum, recorded when its ground truth was drafted. Section 3.2.5 quotes present as the accuracy figure, because it is the only stratum with a corpus-derived ground truth to be accurate against. Dots are the mean paired difference; whiskers are the 95% confidence interval.

  • Mean paired difference (RAG − baseline)
  • 95% confidence interval
  • No difference

Three of four strata clear zero; present does not. Its interval runs from −0.019 to +0.139, so at n = 16 the gain on covered questions is directionally positive but not established. absent_unreleased (n = 4) and absent_by_design (n = 3) reach significance on too few pairs to carry a claim alone.

Every paired difference, one column per query

This is the input to the t-test, sorted by size rather than by query id. Twenty-nine of the forty differences favour retrieval; eleven favour the baseline.

Hover or focus any column for its query id, stratum, response kind and both scores; the full table sits at the foot of this page. The largest single gain, +0.439, is query 31 — an inventory-systems question the archive answers well.

Why present underperforms

Eight of the sixteen covered queries returned the grounded retrieval fallback instead of an answer. Retrieval was not the cause: all eight retrieved the full five context blocks at cosine similarity 0.67–0.74, far above the 0.30 threshold. They were discarded at structural citation validation.

  • Answers (n = 32)
  • Notices (n = 8)

The diagnostics split cleanly along response kind, which is what makes the mechanism measurable rather than speculative. Context Precision is low in both groups and is a separate, still-open retrieval-ranking question.

The mechanism, from the run log

Five of the eight logged Citation repair remained invalid — both the model repair and the deterministic enforce_citation_coverage pass left a substantive unit uncited. The other three logged Citation repair failed (TruncatedGeneration), where the repair call itself hit the 2,000-token output ceiling that applies when no gateway is configured.

The fallback is deliberate: the pipeline discards an answer it cannot cite rather than stapling markers onto uncited claims. What this run adds is the price of that trade on covered questions — and it is the largest single lever on the figure Section 3.2.5 quotes.
